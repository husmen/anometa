Same code, same cached features, three machines: an RTX 3090 (canonical for every reported number), an Apple M4 Pro through PyTorch's MPS backend, and the 3090 host's Ryzen 7 7700 with the GPU hidden. Track B timings come from the 20 frozen configurations on the dev split, one seed at a time; Track A timings come from Vial. Encoder throughput is measured on blank images at each scenario's native size.

  • TabPFN needs no training. Its "fit" stores the context in about 5 ms on every machine. A prediction for one scenario's images takes 0.19 s on the 3090, 0.82 s on the M4 Pro and 1.5 s on the CPU; TabPFN-3.5-Fast is 3 to 4 times faster.
  • The encoder is the real cost. DINOv3-L takes 20 ms per Fabric-sized image on the 3090, 0.23 s on the M4 Pro and 1.1 s on the CPU. For a whole scenario this dominates everything Track B does.
  • Time to a working detector is where the tracks differ most. For all 472 Vial images, the TabPFN pipeline (encode every image, fit, predict) takes about 14 s on the 3090, 2 min on the M4 Pro and 10 min on the CPU. PatchCore takes 18 s, 2.6 min and 2.5 min. EfficientAD-S needs 44 min on the 3090 and, extrapolated from short runs, about 4 h on the M4 Pro and 10 h on the CPU, almost all of it training. So TabPFN's pipeline is slightly faster than PatchCore on the 3090 and the M4 Pro, and far faster than EfficientAD-S everywhere. On the CPU, PatchCore is about 4 times faster, because DINOv3-L at a 512-pixel short side is expensive there; DINOv3-S encodes 10 times faster on the CPU (0.11 s per image) and would close that gap, at some cost in accuracy (dev split).
  • Logistic regression is cheaper still to fit and predict, but it needs its regularisation chosen with extra labels to be calibrated (section 5).
  • PatchCore on the M4 Pro (all 8 scenarios, dev split) matches the 3090 on pixel metrics (AU-PRO@0.05 0.207 vs 0.202), but its image AUROC is 0.03 lower (0.619 vs 0.652), mostly on Fabric. The likely cause is that its random coreset draw differs between MPS and CUDA; the M4 runs also let unsupported MPS operations fall back to the CPU. EfficientAD-S was only timed on the M4 Pro and the CPU, not evaluated.
